# Code of Conduct

We are committed to fostering a welcoming, safe, and collaborative community. All participants are expected to:

- Be respectful, inclusive, and considerate in all interactions.
- Provide constructive feedback and assume positive intent.
- Follow maintainers' directions and abide by project guidelines.
- Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers or via the security contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, or any form of abuse. Repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from the community.

This Code of Conduct is inspired by the Contributor Covenant v2.1.
